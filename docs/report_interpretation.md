# Report interpretation

PROBEAT reports combine quality, taxonomy, annotation, safety, metabolic, and
probiotic-marker evidence. Marker presence indicates genomic potential and
does not demonstrate expression, phenotype, clinical efficacy, or safety.

BUSCO, FastANI, AMR, virulence, plasmid, virus, and probiotic-focused results
must be interpreted together. Warning indicators identify values outside the
configured isolate-genome guidance and are not independent diagnoses.
